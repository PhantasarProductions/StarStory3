# 3rd Party assets, copyrights and AI

I'm reliant on a lot of 3rd party stuff, and I don't want any AI in my assets.
Basically all 3rd party stuff remains copyrighted and licensed by their respective creators.

Now since I do this development most of all alone, it's undoable for me to make 100% sure that all contain has the copyrights and licenses that are claimed and to make sure none of the stuff I get is AI generated. I do a few checks, but I already have a lot of work on the game it self the way it is.
As such, there is a good chance that either some copyrighted stuff that I am not licensed to use or some AI generated stuff did make it past my checks.
If that happens, please write a ticket in the issue tracker and provide me a source I can verify as trustworthy. If an asset is indeed either a copyright violation or an AI generated asset, I'll make sure to replace it as soon as I can.
